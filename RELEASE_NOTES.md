# Apex Pro Live Heatmap 0.1.0-beta

First public beta release.

## Highlights

- Counts physical key presses and renders a live heatmap directly on the keyboard.
- Uses the local SteelSeries GameSense API—no cloud service or external server.
- Includes a matching on-screen preview, adjustable heat decay, optional persistent aggregate counters and tray support.
- Designed and tested for the SteelSeries Apex Pro Full-size Gen 3 with German ISO layout.

## Privacy

The app stores aggregate per-key counts only. It does not store characters, key order, timestamps, active applications, clipboard data or typed text.

## Known limitations

- Other Apex Pro models and layouts have not yet been tested and may need mapping changes.
- Keyboard-internal controls such as the volume wheel or some Fn functions may not produce countable Windows input.
- The executable is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning.
